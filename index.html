How variable time works:

The Fleshler–Hoffman Progression

VT mode generates 12 positive values with the Fleshler–Hoffman progression, scales them to the selected target mean, then shuffles them without replacement. Every complete 12-interval block has exactly the selected arithmetic mean before display rounding.

The progression was designed for variable-interval schedules to approximate a constant opportunity rate over elapsed time. Here, the same time-value progression drives response-independent VT alerts.
